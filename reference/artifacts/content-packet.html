Content packet

Everything the new site needs from you that isn't already on calipercrayon.com or in your CV. Answer in a reply, a doc, voice memo, whatever is easiest; short is fine. Anything you skip stays off the page rather than being guessed.

How the new site works
  • Every project gets its own page with a My role line up top, so a hiring manager sees what you did before what the project was.
  • The text is in your voice, first person. Where I've drafted from your existing copy you'll get to edit it.
  • Photographs of real objects matter more than anything else below. If you only do one thing, do the photo list.
